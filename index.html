Privacy Policy

Nerozon Vendor Application

Last Updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Nerozon ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects the personal and business information of store owners, merchants, authorized personnel, and vendor partners ("Vendors" or "you") using the Nerozon Vendor Application.

By downloading, registering, or managing your store listing through the Nerozon Vendor application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

📌 Overview for Merchants & Vendors: The Nerozon Vendor app is designed to help businesses manage product listings, accept and process customer orders, manage inventory, track earnings, and coordinate local pickups/deliveries seamlessly.

1. Information We Collect

To register your vendor profile, facilitate order management, and enable real-time fulfillment, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Vendor & Account Information: Merchant name, owner/manager full name, business email address, contact phone number, store profile photo, and login credentials.
  • Business & Store Details: Store name, business category, store address, geographic location coordinates (GPS), GST/tax identification numbers, operating hours, and bank account details for payment settlements.
  • Catalog & Product Data: Inventory listings, item names, prices, product descriptions, images, and stock availability updates uploaded to the platform.
  • Order Fulfillment Data: Information regarding customer orders placed with your store, including order history, item quantities, total transaction amounts, and order processing status updates.
  • Device & Usage Technical Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, IP address, app activity logs, and crash diagnostic data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process vendor data to maintain an efficient commercial platform and support your business operations on Nerozon. Specific uses include:

  • Setting up and managing your active vendor profile on the Nerozon platform.
  • Sending real-time order alerts, push notifications, and sound alerts for incoming customer requests.
  • Calculating store payouts, processing daily/weekly earnings settlements, and providing financial statements.
  • Determining store proximity and calculating accurate delivery radiuses for nearby customers and delivery partners.
  • Communicating order updates, platform policy changes, feature updates, and customer service inquiries.
  • Preventing fraud, unauthorized store access, and protecting platform integrity.

3. Device Permissions Requested

To enable operational features within the Nerozon Vendor application, the app may request access to the following device permissions:

  • Location (Foreground & Background): Used to accurately pinpoint store locations on the map, set precise pickup coordinates for delivery partners, and calculate fulfillment routes.
  • Camera & Storage / Media Access: Required to capture and upload store logos, business documents, and product/inventory photos directly from your device.
  • Notifications: Required to issue real-time sound and visual alerts for new orders, customer cancellations, and system updates.
  • Internet & Network Access: Required to keep store inventory, status (Online/Offline), and order status live and in sync with Nerozon systems.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We respect vendor confidentiality. Your data is shared strictly as needed to conduct core business operations:

  • With Customers: Basic store information (store name, store address, phone contact, store images, and active product listings) is published on the Nerozon customer app for browsing and order placement.
  • With Delivery Partners: Pickup address, contact details, and order item details are shared with assigned Nerozon delivery partners to facilitate smooth order pickups.
  • With Service Providers: Trusted third-party service providers (such as cloud hosting, payment gateways, and SMS/notification gateways) who process operational data under strict non-disclosure agreements.
  • For Legal & Regulatory Compliance: When required by government authorities, tax regulations, or lawful summons.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical security measures to protect your sensitive business records, financial credentials, and personal details. All sensitive transmissions between the Vendor App and our servers are encrypted using Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) / Transport Layer Security (TLS) technologies.

6. Account Deletion & Right to Be Forgotten

Vendors have full authority over their account data. If you wish to close your store account and delete your stored vendor records:

  • You can initiate account closure directly inside the Nerozon Vendor App Settings > Account > Delete Account menu.
  • Alternatively, you may submit a formal deletion request via email to support@nerozon.com.

Upon receiving an account deletion request, all personal details, active listings, and store profiles will be permanently erased or anonymized, except where financial, invoice, or tax audit records must be retained as required by statutory law.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect app feature additions, legal changes, or operational enhancements. Significant revisions will be notified via the Vendor App or through email prior to taking effect.
